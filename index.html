Help & Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding certificate validity, processing SLAs, Direct Benefit Transfers, and grievance escalations.

Every certificate generated through the Government Portal is embedded with a high-density 2D QR code and an official 256-bit cryptographic signature. You can verify any document instantly using the DigiCert Verification tool under the Services directory or by scanning the barcode using any camera. Certificates issued under the Information Technology Act carry equal legal standing to physical stamped documents across courts, universities, passport offices, and embassies.
Under the Right to Public Services Act, designated turnaround limits are statutory: Birth and Death Registrations require 3 to 5 business days, Community and Income Certificates require 7 business days, Smart Ration Card modifications require 10 business days, and Patta Land Record extractions are instantaneous. If any application exceeds the SLA threshold, the portal triggers an automated alert to the District Revenue Officer (DRO).
All welfare financial assistance, pensions, scholarship allowances, and input subsidies are credited directly from the state treasury into the beneficiary's Aadhaar-seeded bank account through the Public Financial Management System (PFMS). No physical cash handling, intermediary agents, or processing deductions are permissible at any administrative level.
If an inquiry or civic complaint is not addressed within 48 hours by the respective taluk officer, the portal automatically escalates the ticket to the District Collectorate Camp Office. You will receive an automated SMS update with the designated appellate officer's direct contact details and case review docket number.
Yes. A registered citizen user account allows you to create family member beneficiary profiles. By providing their demographic details and Aadhaar consent, you can submit pension enrollments, healthcare cards, or revenue petitions on behalf of parents, dependents, or elderly relatives without requiring them to visit local administrative counters.
